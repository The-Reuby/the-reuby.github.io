# Submission Guidelines 

# The 'Solastalgia' The Reuby Issue 5

Submissions are open! 

The theme for the next yearly issue is Solastalgia 

## Theme 

### Solastalgia

Solastalgia, a term coined in the 21st century, refers to sadness or melancholy due to environmental change and loss, especially in relation to one’s home. It is a painful sense of nostalgia for the wild, the pristine, and the rural that we feel was a part of us and which we might never again lay eyes upon. Importantly, it is also a call to action – for the collective cherishment and protection of that which is precious but obliviously crushed under the metal and concrete onslaught of modern society, economy, technology, and warfare. A fit subject for our college and its members championing this tumultuous century of ours. So fellow environmentalists and biologists, historians and anthropologists, computer scientists and engineers, poets and photographers, people of all professions, cultures, and walks of life – what will you cherish or mourn, protect or warn against, in this year’s issue of The Reuby? 

The magazine is open to submissions until March 13th, 2027. Submissions can be photos, illustrations, short stories, essays, interviews, academic articles, college-related pieces, poetry, or a mix of all the above. Surprise us.

## Submission Types and Requirements

### Feature Articles
- 300-1200 words
- Preferably of an interdisciplinary nature addressing challenges facing individuals, societies, or the planet in the 21st century
- Content should be accessible to a non-specialized readership
- No need for in-text citations, but if used, provide a list of principal sources

### Art
Art has a unique way of examining important subjects from new angles, confronting moral, psychological, spiritual, and subjective questions. 'The Reuby' seeks new forms and styles alongside existing artistic traditions from around the world.

Mixed art is welcome: photography with illustrations, visual with written, prose poetry, etc. Feature articles or critical reviews about art are also accepted.

**Poetry**
- One piece or up to 3 short, related pieces
- Keep under two pages
- Format: word doc or pdf

**Prose**
- One piece
- Up to 1,500 words
- Format: word doc or pdf

**Visual**
- Up to 5 pieces
- Photography, painting, drawing, graphic illustrations, etc.
- Submissions should be sent digitally. For example, for a canvas painting, send us a photograph or scan at the highest resolution possible.
- Out of all submissions, one piece of visual artwork will be selected to feature on the
issue's cover page. Your submission will be considered unless you explicitly state
otherwise in your email.

### College and University Related Content
- 300-1200 words
- Coverage of events, GCR discussions, OU debates, sports events, and Reubenite life at Oxford
